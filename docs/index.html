Food Insecurity in Rhode Island

Food insecurity in Rhode Island is projected to worsen amid rising grocery prices and federal cuts to SNAP. This site collects recent news, program data, and the sources behind a decision memo for Rhode Island stakeholders and policymakers.

Files in the data folder

  • SNAP persons and benefits, June 2026 (snap_personsjune2026.csv, snap_benefitsjune2026.csv): state-level USDA tables.
  • FY25 and FY26 workbooks (FY25.xlsx, FY26.xlsx): USDA national and state monthly and annual data.
  • Federal Register rules, 2025: final and proposed rules from USDA (115 matching, 100 retrieved) and HHS (174 matching, 100 retrieved), collected on October 13, 2026 through the Federal Register API, with coded versions and codebooks.
  • RI SNAP and food access documents: the 25 collected documents, their codebook, and the coded and evaluated versions.
  • Food Insecurity in Rhode Island 2024: dataset requested from Feeding America. USDA has cancelled its own food insecurity data collection.
